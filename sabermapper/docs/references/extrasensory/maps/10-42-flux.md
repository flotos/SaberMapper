## Concept

Time and eternity. The description asks whether time exists to a being of eternity; the difficulty
labels are `ETERNITY` (Expert) and `CATACLYSMIA` (Expert+). Assets name the imagery directly:
`clockscene.prefab`, `monitorscene.prefab`, `filmgrain.mat`, `blackout.mat`, `pixelsort.mat`.

## What is distinctive

**The widest event vocabulary in the pack — eleven distinct Vivify event types**, against a pack median
of six. Every other map specialises; this one uses the whole instrument. Given the author, that reads
as intentional demonstration.

**The most camera work in EXSII by a wide margin:** 4 `CreateCamera`, 14 `CreateScreenTexture`, 11
`SetCameraProperty`, 6 `SetRenderingSettings`. Multiple simultaneous render targets composited
together — the `monitorscene` prefab implies screens showing other cameras' output.

**Setup begins before the song.** Events appear in the negative-beat window (a `SetRenderingSettings`
and an `InstantiatePrefab` before beat 0). The scene is standing before the first note is possible.

**Both sabers possessed, the head free.** Two `AssignPlayerToTrack` at beat 0 onto `saberzzz1` →
`LeftHand` and `saberzzz2` → `RightHand`. Same split as `End Times`: the hands become scene objects,
the camera stays put. At 225 BPM and 10.7 NPS that is the only safe choice — moving the camera here
would be unplayable.

**The show does scale with difficulty, slightly.** Expert+ adds 110 `AssignPathAnimation` and 7
`AnimateTrack` calls. Every Vivify event count is identical; only the note-track animations differ,
because there are 218 more notes to animate. This is the cleanest evidence in the pack that **the
visual program is difficulty-independent and note animation is not** — the delta is exactly the notes.

**`pointDefinitions` as shared curves.** Seven named definitions reused across 1400+ animation events.
This is the authoring pattern that makes a program this large maintainable, and it is the only map
that uses it.

**Info.dat is schema 2.0.0 while the difficulties are 3.2.0.** Legal, and a parsing trap: the Info
version does not predict the difficulty version.

## What to take from it

- **Named, reusable animation curves (`pointDefinitions`) should be a first-class concept in our
  arrangement format.** Aeroluna, who wrote the mod, uses them; nobody else in the pack does. For a
  generative pipeline emitting thousands of animation events, shared curves are the difference between
  a maintainable output and an unreadable one.
- Multi-camera composition (`CreateCamera` + `CreateScreenTexture` + `SetCameraProperty`) is the
  highest-ceiling technique available and the least used. Screens showing other views of the scene.
- **Possess the hands, not the head, when the chart is fast.** The two densest maps in the pack that
  use possession at all (`42-flux`, `End Times`) both leave the camera alone. Our composer should
  refuse to emit camera possession above some density threshold.
- Pre-song setup in negative beats is standard practice and our compiler must allow negative event
  times.
- The difficulty delta being *exactly* the note-animation events is a validation rule we can implement:
  when generating a downmap, the Vivify event set should be unchanged and only note-bound animation
  should shrink.
- At 6.30 NPS on Expert+ this is the second-densest map in the pack and the closest to the player's
  historical band in `PLAYER.md` after `Yoi Okashi` — while still carrying 1602 events. It is the best
  single demonstration that spectacle and real difficulty are compatible.

## How the idea follows from the song

**Song.** 42-flux by rN vs. nm-y (Cataclysmia), a fast, dense 225 BPM instrumental written for
the BMS event BOFXVII, with no lyrics. Aeroluna's description asks whether time exists for an
eternal being; the labels are `ETERNITY` and `CATACLYSMIA`.

**Verified in the files (Expert+).** Both hands possessed at beat 0 (`LeftHand`, `RightHand`), with no
later `AnimateTrack` on those tracks: possession `hands`. `clockscene` spawns at beat -1 and a film
grain Blit at 0; notes run 48–570. Scenes replace each other: monitor 48, wormhole 112, eclipse and sun
with a nine-pass bloom 144, desert 176.5, library with a wingdings overlay 208, portal 272, cylinder
301 (832 `AnimateTrack` in 300–367), screen scene with a black hole 364, per-eye note prefabs 422,
second library 432, icosphere 496, second monitor 538 with a red `_Danger` wingdings screen,
icosphere rain 554, podium with volumetric fog and bloom 570. Notes per 16 beats reach 53, 48, 43 in
496–543, the densest of the map.

**Interpretation.** The clock opens the map; everything after is a tour through cosmic and archival
spaces (wormhole, eclipse, black hole, library) that reads as time coming apart. Motifs: monitors
and screens, bloom light, geometric forms, glyph text. The monitor from beat 48 comes back at 538,
now red and in danger, during the densest writing; that return is held for the end. Hands, not head:
at 225 BPM the camera stays fixed.
