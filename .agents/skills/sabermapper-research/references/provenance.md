# Phrase record and source rules

Primary guidance: [BSMG basic mapping](https://bsmg.wiki/mapping/basic-mapping.html), [intermediate mapping](https://bsmg.wiki/mapping/intermediate-mapping.html), [mapping glossary](https://bsmg.wiki/mapping/glossary.html), [basic lighting](https://bsmg.wiki/mapping/basic-lighting.html), and [map format](https://bsmg.wiki/mapping/map-format.html). Ranking rules from [ScoreSaber](https://wiki.scoresaber.com/ranking/criteria) are distinct from this player's artistic preferences.

Compact phrase record fields: `id`, source URL/path, map SHA-256, map author/version, song/audio SHA-256 or unknown, characteristic/difficulty, beat start/end, meter/BPM/offset evidence, notes or source pointer, entry/exit hand state, musical role, movement intent, review status, rights status, and one fit/counterfit sentence. Keep raw source arrays out of the target-song authoring context when evaluating against a human map of that same song.

Mechanical faults (bad coordinates, impossible overlaps, malformed map JSON) differ from contextual warnings (parity, clutter, NJS, repetition) and personal taste. A source map can demonstrate an interesting technical idea without establishing a universal rule. Note the mapper/player/style era before generalizing.
